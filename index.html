About
Privacy. Everything you enter stays on this device; there is no account and nothing is uploaded. The only outside request is an exchange-rate lookup (frankfurter.dev, European Central Bank rates) when you enter a price in a currency other than GBP. It sends no coffee data, but like any web request it reveals your IP address. Backups are plain, unencrypted files.
